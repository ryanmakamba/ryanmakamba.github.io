About me

The work makes sense when the decision does.

01

How it started

My career began in Zimbabwe’s insurance sector, working across claims, client risk, coverage, pricing and insurer negotiations. It taught me early that analysis is only valuable when it improves a real business decision.

02

The perspective expanded

Institutional research at Quinnipiac and fundraising data work with The Salvation Army widened that view. I learned how reliable data, careful reconciliation and clear reporting support decisions across very different organizations.

03

Right now

I’m completing graduate study in Applied AI and Business Analytics while building practical systems around underwriting, claims intelligence, model explainability and data governance.
